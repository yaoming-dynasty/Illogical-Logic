## 3. The Three-Phase Composition Protocol

The Composition Bridge executes synthesis through a precise three-step protocol:

### Phase 1: Exterior Aggregation (Gathering from $O$)
Disparate data points, transactions, or signals are collected from `{The_Outside_Boundary}` ($O$). In decentralized finance or multi-party computation, this is the gathering of public inputs or rollup blocks.

### Phase 2: Interface Verification & Filtering (At $L$)
The aggregated batch hits `{The_Boundary_LIne}` ($L$). Here, cryptographic verification (such as smart contract execution or zero-knowledge proof checks) validates that all incoming exterior states comply with the system's structural rules via $\sim$.

### Phase 3: Interior Recomposition (Sealing into $I$)
Once verified, the components fuse into a new, single sovereign state inside `{The_Boundary_Inside}` ($I$). The system's internal complexity increases while preserving its overall structural boundary.
